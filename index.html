Your life, on your device.

AragonTask is a local-first Android app that combines todos, an expense ledger, and memos in one calm, paper-textured app — with an optional AI agent powered by your own API key.

Todos that keep up

Quick-add, colored categories, due dates, a today progress ring, and repeating tasks that auto-spawn the next occurrence.

Ledger built for real life

Day-grouped records, reimbursement tracking (pending → submitted → reimbursed), and monthly budget alerts.

📊

Stats you'll actually read

Monthly summary, category share bars, and a 6-month expense trend — hand-drawn SVG, no chart libs.

AI agent, your keys

Plug in OpenAI / Anthropic / custom endpoints, then chat to manage data and search the web. No key? 100% offline.

🔍

Find anything

Global search across todos, memos and records with jump-to-item highlighting.

Own your data
